## 1. The Short Version

## 2. Information You Give Us

### Your account

When you create an account, we collect your **email address** and a **password**. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your password in a protected (hashed) form. We never see or store your password ourselves. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us so we can set up your account. If you sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name (the first time you sign in, and only if you choose to share it) and either your email address or, if you choose Hide My Email, a private relay address that forwards to your email. We use it in the same way as an email address you give us, and we never see your real address unless you share it.

### Your profile

We collect the **display name** and **@username** you choose, and, if you add one, your **profile photo**. The App crops and shrinks the photo on your iPhone before uploading it, and we store that small version on our servers. You can also make your account **private**, so that you approve each person who follows you.

### Your phone number (optional)

If you add a phone number so friends can find you from their contacts, Google Firebase verifies it by sending a code by text message. Once it is verified, we store the number on our servers and in your Firebase account. You can remove it at any time in Settings, which removes it from both.

### Your birthday (optional)

If you add your birthday in Settings, we store your **date of birth** on our servers, linked to your account, so that it is kept when you reinstall the App or sign in on another iPhone. We use it only to check that you are old enough to use Marquee (the App does not accept a birthday that makes you younger than 13) and its AI features, and to work out the age range (such as 25–34) sent with AI Discover requests. Only you can see your birthday: it is never shown to other users, and it is never sent to our AI provider or anyone else. You can change or remove it at any time in Settings, which removes it from our servers.

### What you add to Marquee

We store the content you create and the things you do in the App, including:

- Your lists, the films and TV shows in them, their rankings, and each list's name, description and visibility
- Films you have marked as watched, your watch diary (the dates you watched films, including rewatches) and the TV episodes you have marked as watched
- Notes you write about films
- Recommendations you send to and receive from other users, including any message you add
- People you follow, people who follow you, follow requests, and people you have blocked
- Lists you follow, and lists you have invited others to edit or been invited to edit
- Movie night groups you create or belong to, and the films, reactions and votes in them
- The Terms of Service version you accepted and when, and that you confirmed you are at least 13
- Reports you make about other users or their content: who and what you reported, the reason you picked and any details you add

### Your settings

We store your notification choices, the streaming services you use and your country for streaming purposes, whether you have allowed AI features, and records of which streaming, release and new season alerts we have already sent you, so you do not get the same one twice.

### When you contact us

If you email us, we collect your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it to respond to you.

## 3. Information Collected Automatically

- **Notification tokens.** If you allow notifications, Apple and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging give the App a token for your device, which we store with your account so we can send you notifications. When you sign out, we remove it.
- **Technical information.** Like any online service, our servers and hosting providers receive technical information when the App connects to them, such as your IP address, the time of the request and the type of request. Our hosting providers keep server logs for a limited time for security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. We use IP addresses and account identifiers briefly, in our server's memory only, to limit how many requests someone can make in a short time (for example, how often someone can check whether a @username is taken), to protect the App from abuse.
- **Usage analytics.** The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count which features are used, as described below.
- **Crash reports.** The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to tell us when it crashes, as described below.
- **AI usage counts.** To keep AI features fair and affordable, we count how many AI requests each account makes per day. The count is stored in our database with your account, is deleted after a few days, and is not stored long term.

We do not use cookies in the App. We do not collect your device's advertising identifier, and the App does not ask for permission to track you.

### Usage analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how Marquee is used so we can fix problems and improve it. It collects:

- A random **app instance identifier** created when the App is installed. It is not your device's advertising identifier, and it changes if you delete and reinstall the App
- **Events** recording that you used certain features: signing up or signing in (and the method), accepting the Terms, creating your first and later lists, adding, ranking or marking a film as watched (and where in the App you did it, such as Discover or search), using an AI feature, sending a recommendation (and roughly to how many people), opening a Theatre Check-in, sharing a list, your stats or your Year in Review (and whether the share was completed), importing lists (the source and roughly how many films), whether you allowed notifications, when the App asked you to rate it, and sending a report (only the type of content and the reason you picked). Events never include film or list names, notes, messages, @usernames or anything else you type
- **Device and App information**, such as the device model, iOS version, App version, language and time zone
- **Approximate location** (country, region and city), which Google works out from your IP address. The App does not send your device's location
- **Session information**, such as when the App is first opened, how often and for how long it is used, and when it is updated

We do not set a user ID in Google Analytics, so this information is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or account. The App uses the version of Google Analytics that does not collect your device's advertising identifier, and we have turned off ad personalisation signals and Google's collection of the identifier for vendors. We do not use analytics for advertising, and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google processes this information on our behalf as our service provider.

Analytics are on unless you turn them off. You can turn them off at any time with **Share Usage & Crash Data** in Settings, after which the App stops sending analytics. The setting applies to this iPhone.

### Crash reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, so we can find and fix crashes. When the App crashes, it sends a crash report the next time it is opened. A crash report includes:

- A **crash log**: where in the App's code the crash happened and what the App was doing at the time, such as the screen's state and the code that was running
- **Device and App information**, such as the device model, iOS version, orientation, free memory and disk space, App version and build, and whether the device is jailbroken
- A random **installation identifier** created by Crashlytics, so we can tell how many installations a crash affects. It is not your device's advertising identifier, and it changes if you delete and reinstall the App

We do not set a user ID or any other personal information in Crashlytics, so crash reports are not linked to your name, email address, phone number or account, and they do not include your films, lists, messages or anything else you type. Google processes crash reports on our behalf as our service provider and keeps them for 90 days.

Crash reports are controlled by the same **Share Usage & Crash Data** setting as analytics. They are on unless you turn the setting off, after which the App stops sending crash reports.

## 4. Information That Stays on Your iPhone

Some features work only on your device. This information is not sent to us, except as described here:

- **Discover.** The films you like, skip or mark "not for me" in Discover, your Discover filters, and the taste profile built from them are stored on your iPhone. They are sent to our servers only when you use AI Picks, so that the AI can make suggestions (see Section 7).
- **Pronouns and age range.** If you choose your pronouns for better recommendations, they are stored on your iPhone. Your birthday is stored with your account (see Section 2), and the App also keeps a copy of it on your iPhone, where it works out your age range (such as 25–34). Only that age range, and your pronouns, are sent with AI Discover requests, and only while AI features are on; your birthday itself is never sent to our AI provider. If you chose an age range in an earlier version of the App and have not added a birthday, that age range stays on your iPhone and is used in the same way. If your birthday or age range shows you are under 18, the App turns AI features off for you.
- **Daylight appearance.** If you choose the Daylight appearance, the App uses your approximate location, rounded to about 10 kilometres, to work out sunrise and sunset. It is stored on your iPhone and is not sent to us.
- **Theatre check-ins.** If you turn on Theatre Check-ins, the App uses your iPhone's location, including in the background and with precise location, to notice when you spend about a film's length at a movie theatre and then leave. The theatres near you, when you arrived and where you are stay on your iPhone. To find nearby theatres, the App asks Apple Maps, which means Apple receives your approximate location under Apple's own privacy policy. If you then choose what you saw, we receive only the film and the date, like any other diary entry, and not the theatre or your location.
- **Widgets.** To show your top films and tonight's suggestions on your Home Screen, the App saves a small copy of that information in a space on your iPhone shared with its widgets. It is removed when you sign out.
- **Offline copy.** The App keeps a copy of your lists and some settings on your iPhone so it can work quickly and offline. It is removed when you sign out.
- **Imports and exports.** When you import a Letterboxd export or a spreadsheet, the file is read on your iPhone, and only the lists and films in it (such as titles, years, rankings, watched dates and notes) are sent to our servers to look up the films and add them to your account. Imported notes and watched dates are saved like ones you add in the App. Marquee does not connect to your Letterboxd account. When you export your lists, the file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to share it.

## 6. Finding Friends From Your Contacts

If you choose to find friends from your contacts, the App reads the email addresses and phone numbers in your iPhone's contacts and turns each one into a one-way code (a SHA-256 hash) on your iPhone. Only those codes are sent to us. We compare them with codes made in the same way from our users' email addresses and verified phone numbers, tell you which of your contacts are on Marquee, and then discard the codes you sent. We do not store them, and names and other contact details never leave your iPhone.

This also means that other users who have your email address, or your phone number if you have added one, in their contacts may be able to find your Marquee profile. You can remove your phone number in Settings at any time.

## 7. AI Features

AI features (AI Picks in Discover, AI suggestions for Movie Night, and Smart Placement in ranked lists) are **off by default**. They only work if you turn on **Allow AI Features** in Settings, and they are not available to users who tell us they are under 18. We store whether you have allowed them on our servers, so that a friend's Movie Night can check that everyone has agreed before anyone's lists are used.

When you use an AI feature, our servers send our AI provider, Anthropic, the information needed to make suggestions. Depending on the feature, this can include:

- The names of your lists and the titles, years, original languages, rankings and watched status of the films and shows in them (for Movie Night, only lists that are not private)
- The films you liked, skipped or marked "not for me" in Discover, how many you liked, and the films already suggested
- Your genre preferences and filters, your chosen streaming services, and your country and language
- The age range worked out from your birthday, and your pronouns, if you chose to share them (Discover only). Never your birthday itself
- For Movie Night, the same kind of list information for each friend you are planning with, identified by their @username, and the films your group has liked, turned down or shortlisted

We do not send your name, email address, phone number, notes, photo or contacts. Anthropic processes this information on our behalf to generate suggestions. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic may not use it to train its models, and it keeps it only for a limited time. AI suggestions we show you, such as the reason a film was picked, may be saved with your lists or groups.

You can turn AI features off at any time in Settings. We then stop sending your information to Anthropic.

## 13. Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account at any time in Settings by tapping **Delete Account**. For your security, you first confirm it is you by entering your password or signing in with Google or Apple again, and our servers only accept a deletion request made shortly after that. Your account is then deleted in a single step on our servers:

- Your account, profile, photo, birthday, phone number, lists, rankings, watched history, diary, episode progress, notes, follows, follow requests, blocks, recommendations you have sent and received, lists you follow, reports you have made and reports about your account, notification tokens, alert records and settings are deleted from our database
