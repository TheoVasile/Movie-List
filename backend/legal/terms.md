## 10. Location Features

Two optional features use your iPhone's location. Both are off unless you turn them on, and both rely on the permissions you grant in iOS.

### Daylight appearance

If you choose the Daylight appearance, the App switches between light and dark themes at sunrise and sunset. To work out those times, it uses your approximate location, rounded to about 10 kilometres and stored on your iPhone. It is not sent to us.

### Theatre check-ins

If you turn on Theatre Check-ins, the App uses Apple's location services, including in the background ("Always" permission and precise location), to recognise when you spend roughly a film's length at a movie theatre and then leave, and sends you a notification asking what you saw. To do this, the App uses Apple Maps to look up movie theatres near you and asks your iPhone to watch the nearest ones. These searches are handled by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Where you go is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us. The feature works with any theatre Apple Maps knows about and depends on Apple Maps data and iOS location accuracy, so it may miss a visit or prompt you when you did not see a film.

### Turning location off

You can turn either feature off in Marquee's Settings at any time, and you can change or withdraw location permission for Marquee in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy and Security, Location Services. The rest of the App works without location access.
